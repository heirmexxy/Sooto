Real money. Nothing anyone will believe.

Your income moves through two banks, a wallet, M-Pesa or MoMo, and cash. No lender sees all of it, so the answer is no, or a rate that charges you for being invisible.

So you print months of statements, hand a stranger every transaction you have ever made, and still wait to be believed.

Honest people should not have to expose their whole lives to be trusted, while forged statements get waved through.

Someone changed ₦450,000 to ₦950,000. The balance beside it no longer adds up. Most people reading a PDF never notice.

Edited statements and scams make every lender suspicious of everyone. Social engineering made up 47% of digital payment fraud by volume in Nigeria in 2025, according to NIBSS.

Built for the people the system cannot see.

Sooto is built in Nigeria, for traders, farmers, founders, freelancers and students whose real business never shows up where a lender looks.

We think proving your money should take one honest answer, not your whole financial history. So Sooto checks the statement the way an auditor would, and shares only what you choose.

  • An open standard. Proofs follow Open Financial Proof, a specification anyone can implement and check, free.
  • Every balance checked. Each line is added to the one before it, so edited amounts, inserted rows and parked money stand out.
  • Reads what banks actually send. PDF and CSV statements, password-protected PDFs, M-Pesa and MTN MoMo.
  • Verified names. Link your BVN or NIN through a licensed KYC provider. Sooto keeps the name, never the number.
  • Open source. The code is public under the MIT licence, so anyone can see exactly what it does.

Three steps to a proof anyone can check.

Do it yourself in a few minutes, or ask your AI assistant to do it for you.

  1. Give Sooto your statement

    Upload the PDF or CSV your bank or wallet already gives you, password and all. It goes straight from your browser, never through a chat.

  2. Sooto checks every line

    Balances are added up, edits and borrowed money are flagged, and scam text in narrations is ignored. Add your BVN to put a verified name on it.

  3. Send a proof, not your statement

    Choose who it is for and why. They get a signed answer that expires, and can check it in one call.
